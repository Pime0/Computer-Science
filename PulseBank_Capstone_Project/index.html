📐 Discrete Math Formula — CSC 413
M = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1] Where: M = Monthly repayment P = Principal (loan amount) r = Monthly rate = annual% / 12 / 100 n = Number of months Geometric Series (Discrete Math): Sum = a(1-r^n)/(1-r) Applied to loan amortization ✓ Example (₦500,000, 12%, 36 months): r = 0.12/12 = 0.01 M = 500000 × [0.01(1.01)^36] / [(1.01)^36 - 1] M = ₦16,607.15 per month
